VecturaBook — English

Address book and clients

Clients and their addresses, plus your own places. The book grows automatically whenever you enter a new client in a trip.

1/4

Address Book

The "Clients" tab holds clients and their addresses; the "Places" tab holds your own locations. Hidden clients are not suggested when picking a client; "Show hidden" brings them back into the list.

2/4

Clients

"New client" adds a client. A client that has trips cannot be deleted, but you can choose "Remove from picker". Past trips keep their client name.

3/4

Client

Name, driver notes, and addresses. When you rename a client, the app checks whether that name is already in the book. If you delete a client, you can immediately reverse it by tapping "Undo".

4/4

Address

Address, label, and coordinates. You can save the coordinates from your current location. "Open in navigator" and "Send" share the location. A red mark highlights an address to watch out for (tight entrance, special site rules).
