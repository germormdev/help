Shift

A shift is your working day from start to finish. Trips, refuels, and parking stops happen inside a shift; shift totals go into your history and reports.

1/4

Start Shift

Tap "Start Shift". The app greets you and shows where you are: your city is detected from your location if you allowed it, otherwise tap the line with the pin and type the place yourself.

If you do not have a trip yet, tap "Waiting for Trip" to start the waiting timer. "Stop Waiting" stops it. Pre-trip waiting time is recorded in your history and report.

When you have a trip, tap "Start Trip".

2/4

Shift status

The "Shift" screen shows whether the shift is open, when it was opened, and how many trips are in it. "Resume shift" takes you back to work.

You cannot close a shift while a trip is still in progress: finish the trip first.

3/4

End Shift

The red "End Shift" button is the only red button in the app. The app will ask you to confirm and enter the final odometer reading. The number is checked: if it is lower than the previous reading or much higher than expected, the app shows the last known reading and asks you to confirm or fix it.

4/4

Forgotten shift

If a shift has been open for over a day, the app offers to close it using the time of the last event — that way your working time in the report is not inflated. You can agree or continue the shift.
