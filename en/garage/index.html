Garage: trucks and trailers

Garage stores your vehicles. It provides the rig drawings, plate numbers, trailer compatibility, mileage, and all vehicle settings.

1/8

Garage

Two tabs: "Trucks" and "Trailers". The active vehicle is highlighted. "+ Add Truck" and "+ Add Trailer" add a new one. Tapping a vehicle opens its management screen.

While a trip is in progress, you cannot change vehicles in Garage — finish the trip first (to change trailers on the road, use "Swap Trailer").

2/8

Truck

Plate number, truck type ("Semi Truck" or "Rigid"), and body type — chosen through a chain of picture questions — plus whether the truck has a crane or manipulator. You cannot have two vehicles with the same plate number in Garage.

3/8

Trailer

Plate number, trailer type (semi-trailer or rigid trailer), and body type. For a fuel tanker or tanker: number of sections and volume of each; for a car transporter: number of car slots; for a refrigerated trailer: whether a generator (genset) is installed.

4/8

Vehicle management

The vehicle screen brings everything about it together: "Registration", "Documents", "Maintenance", "Inspection", "Axles and tyres", "Number plate", and "Paint". "Make Active" selects it for work.

5/8

Vehicle settings

Current odometer, service interval (kilometers, months, warning thresholds), starting point for the service countdown, axles, fuel tank volume, and your critical reserve (for the "to reserve" estimate). For a trailer with a unit: unit tank and unit critical reserve.

6/8

Number plate

Plate background color and number color — just like on your real vehicle. If the combination is hard to read, the number is shown in black or white.

7/8

Paint

Colors of the cab, crane, and body on the vehicle drawing. "As drawn" restores the original color.

8/8

Axles and tyres

The axle diagram is drawn automatically from the number of axles; any axle can be switched between "Single" and "Dual". Tap a tyre to log a "Tyre change", "Puncture", "Rotation", or "Something else" — the entry is saved to History. "Statistics" shows the average distance between tyre changes and the most frequent position. A trailer has its own mileage, which you can enter.
