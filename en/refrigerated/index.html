Refrigerated

A refrigerated vehicle carries temperature-controlled cargo.

1/3

In Garage

Body type "Refrigerated"; check "Generator (genset) installed" if it has one, and enter the "Unit tank, l" and "Unit critical reserve, l".

2/3

On a trip

"Temperature-controlled?": "Controlled" or "Ambient", plus cargo temperature. On the driving screen, use the "Trailer unit running" switch.

3/3

Unit fuel

Enter trailer unit diesel in its own field when refueling; the app tracks unit hours and consumption in L/h and shows "≈ N h to reserve".
