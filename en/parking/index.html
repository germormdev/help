Parking and rest

A parking stop is any break on the road: rest, overnight stay, or waiting. It is logged in the trip with its own address and time.

1/3

Parking / Stop

On the driving screen, tap "Parking / Stop". You can enter the address by hand, pick from saved places, or tap the GPS icon. If the address cannot be found, the app fills in the coordinates — you can edit them.

Nights in the cabin are counted in your history and report.

2/3

Places

Places are your own locations not tied to any client: truck stops, ports, depots, fuel stations. You can search the list by name. Places can be selected when parking and when returning a container.

3/3

Place

Place name, note, and coordinates. You can save the coordinates from your current location, replace them, or erase them. The "Open in navigator" and "Send" buttons pass the location to your navigation app or a message.
