Period Report

Choose a period: "Today", "Week", "Month", or "Custom" dates. Cards show the totals. "Export report" creates a PDF with shifts, trips, stops, and refuels that you can share or print.

What is in the PDF

For each shift, the PDF lists the start and end times, truck and trailer, trips with clients, cargo, unloading and waiting times, odometers, and refuels. At the end are the "Period totals": shifts, trips, refuels, total distance, total duration, total fuel, average distance per shift, and average consumption.
