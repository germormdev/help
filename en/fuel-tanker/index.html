Fuel Tanker

A fuel tanker carries fuel in separate sections.

1/3

In Garage

Body type "Fuel Tanker"; "Number of Sections" and the volume of each section in liters.

2/3

On a trip

In "Cargo Details", enter the volume for each section.

3/3

Unloading

At each stop, select the unloaded sections. If any sections are still not delivered when you finish the trip, the app will ask you to confirm.
