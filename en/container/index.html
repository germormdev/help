Container

A container chassis carries one or two 20ft, 40ft, or 45ft containers.

1/3

In Garage

Body type "Container", plus the "Container Size" the trailer is built for.

2/3

On a trip

Chain of questions: "Empty or laden?", "Container size?", "How many containers?", "Container type?" ("Standard", Reefer, Flat Rack, Open Top, Tank). Scan the container number and seal number with the camera. Venta seal number and temperature are available for Reefer.

3/3

After unloading

The app asks what happened to the container: left with the client, still on the truck, going to another client, or returning to a port or depot.
