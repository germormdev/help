Maintenance and tyres

Maintenance: service records, intervals, and reminders when service is due.

1/3

Maintenance

At the top, you see how many kilometers or days remain until the next service, or by how much it is overdue. Below are your service records. Intervals and the starting point are set in the vehicle settings.

2/3

Service record

"Log service" opens a new record: odometer, date, what was done, and photos. The odometer at service cannot be higher than the current odometer.

3/3

Tyres

Tyres are tracked on the axle diagram — see the "Garage" section, "Axles and tyres" step.
