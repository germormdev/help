Trip history and editing

History holds all your shifts and trips. Here you can view, search, edit, and delete them.

1/3

History

Trips are grouped by shift: shift start and end, duration, mileage, and number of trips. Tap a trip to see its details: client, cargo, stops, odometers, waiting time, and photos. Search by client, container, or route. "All trips" shows them in a single list.

2/3

Edit trip

"Edit" opens the same steps as when creating a trip, plus the times and points screen. Your changes are collected and saved only on the summary screen.

If an odometer edit affects other records (the next trip, the truck reading, or shift mileage), the app shows them before applying the change.

3/3

Delete

Delete a single trip with the trash icon, or delete several using selection mode. An active trip cannot be deleted — finish it first. If your phone is connected to the online app, the deletion will sync to your other devices as well.
