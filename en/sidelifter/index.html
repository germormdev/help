Sidelifter

A sidelifter loads and unloads containers by itself.

1/3

In Garage

The "Sidelifter" body type is only available for a semi-trailer.

2/3

On a trip

Enter the container the same way as for a container chassis; mark "Working with crane" on the trip summary.

3/3

Exchange

A sidelifter often swaps containers at a stop — use "Container Exchange".
