VecturaBook — English

Documents and expiry dates

Vehicle and driver documents with page photos and expiry dates. The app reminds you before they expire.

1/5

All documents

The overview lists the documents for all vehicles and the driver in one place with their expiry status: how many days are left or "expired".

2/5

Vehicle Documents

Each vehicle has its own list: "Vehicle registration", "Insurance", "Inspection", and custom documents. Tap "+" to add a document.

3/5

Document

Pick a document type or "Custom document…" and enter a name (for example, ADR permit). "Valid until" is the expiry date used for reminders. An inspection also has a "Passed on" date.

4/5

Document pages

Take photos of the document pages with the camera. You can open pages full-screen, swipe through them, and delete them one by one.

5/5

Expiry reminders

Before a document expires, the app sends a notification and shows the "Document expiry" dialog when you open the app. "Got it" dismisses the dialog until the next reminder threshold. Driver documents ("Driver's License", "Passport", "ADR Certificate") are in "Profile Settings".
