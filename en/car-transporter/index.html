Car Transporter

A car transporter carries passenger vehicles.

1/3

In Garage

Body type "Car Transporter"; "Number of car slots".

2/3

On a trip

Add vehicles by VIN (17 characters, no I, O, or Q) or by "License plate"; you can scan the VIN with the camera. A counter shows how many vehicles are loaded.

3/3

Unloading

At each stop, mark which vehicles were dropped and which were picked up. If vehicles are still on board when you finish the trip, the app will ask you to confirm.
