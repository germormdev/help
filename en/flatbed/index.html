Flatbed

A flatbed carries general and oversized cargo.

1/3

In Garage

Select the "Flatbed" body type and enter the "Flatbed length".

2/3

On a trip

Cargo types: "General" (pallets, weight, description), "Oversized" (length, width, height, weight), or "Container" on a flatbed. For oversized cargo, the "Load details" step asks for the dimensions.

3/3

Unloading

Pallets are unloaded at each stop by count or by percentage.
