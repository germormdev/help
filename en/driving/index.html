Driving screen

While a trip is underway, the Main Screen shows "In progress": the vehicle on the trip, truck and trailer plate numbers, driving time, and buttons for your next actions.

1/3

In progress

Buttons on the screen: "Arrived" (you are at the stop), "Refuel", and "Parking / Stop". Above the buttons is a drawing of your rig with its plate numbers, built from what is saved in Garage: type, body, axles, and colors.

If you entered the fuel tank volume and your critical reserve in Garage, you will see the estimated distance or time to reserve below the truck: "≈ N km to reserve" for truck diesel and "≈ N h to reserve" for the trailer unit (reefer, genset). This is calculated from your refuels and mileage, so it may not appear right after your very first refuel.

2/3

Swap Trailer

To change trailers on the road, tap "Swap Trailer". The window shows the trailers from your Garage: a drawing of each trailer with its plate number underneath. Pick the new one and the swap is logged in the trip. Trailers that are not compatible with your truck are not shown.

3/3

Odometer

The app fills in the expected reading: from the truck, from loading, from the last drop, or from the pump. Check it and fix it if needed. You can also scan the odometer with the camera.

If the number is lower than the previous one, much higher than expected, or left untouched, the app shows the last known reading and asks if it is right. Editing the odometer in a past trip shows which other records will be affected.
