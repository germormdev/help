VecturaBook — English

Settings

Settings are organized into groups: "Driver profile", "Phone & connection", "Documents (PDF)", "Backup", and "General settings".

1/10

Settings

Here you will find "Language & Region", units, "Theme", "Animations", "Fuel PDF" and "Trip history PDF", "Backup", and connection to the online app.

2/10

Driver profile

Your name for reports and driver documents: "Driver's License", "Passport", and "ADR Certificate" — with expiry dates and page photos.

3/10

Language & Region

"App language", "GPS addresses" language ("Auto (by current country)"), and "Date Format".

4/10

Units

"Distance", "Weight", "Volume", "Length / Size", "Temperature", and "Fuel consumption". Presets: "Metric", "Imperial", "Mixed", or "Custom".

5/10

Theme

"Light", "Dark", or "Auto" (by "Day starts" and "Night starts" times). Presets: "Kraft", "Dark Kraft", "Manila", "Almost White", "Dark". You can also build your own.

6/10

Custom theme

Colors for "Background", "Text", "Top bar", "Card", "Field border", "Captions", "Buttons and highlight", "Refuel icon", and "Parking icon". You can save a theme, rename it, and assign it to Light or Dark mode.

7/10

Animations

"Moving scenes for unloading and refuelling". You can turn them off.

8/10

About

App version, "Website", Telegram, Facebook, and a way to "Support the project". The app was formerly called CargoLog — only the name has changed.

9/10

Feedback / Bug Report

Send a "Bug Report", a "Suggestion", or "Other" message. You can attach a screenshot or photo. Also in the menu: "Check for updates" and "Rate the app".

10/10

Help

"Help" is the table of contents of this manual: "General" sections and sections "By vehicle type". Each one opens right here, step by step.
