Trip: loading to unloading

A trip is logged step by step: origin and destination, what you are carrying, cargo details, summary — and "Start trip". At the stop: arrival, unloading or loading, summary. Until you tap "Start trip", nothing is written to the logbook: at any step you can go back with the "Back" button without losing what you entered.

1/10

Client & Route

Select a client: start typing the name and the app will suggest matches from the Address Book. For a new client, just type the name — it will be added to the book automatically.

A client can have several addresses — pick the one you need or add a new one. The GPS icon fills in the address from your current location. Driver notes about this client are shown right here.

2/10

What are you carrying?

You pick the cargo type using tiles: Container, General, Oversized, Refrigerated, Fuel Tanker, Tipper, Car Transporter, Empty. The list depends on the body type of your truck and trailer.

If you change the cargo type after entering details, the app warns you that your entries will be erased and asks you to confirm.

3/10

Cargo Details

The details depend on the cargo type. For a container: empty or laden, size, how many containers, type, container number, and seal number. Numbers can be scanned with the camera. For tankers: sections and volumes; for a car transporter: vehicles by VIN or license plate; for general cargo: pallets and weight.

If no cargo details are needed for the trip (for example, when running empty), simply tap "Next" (or "Continue").

The ADR switch opens the UN number fields for dangerous goods.

4/10

Load details

For oversized cargo and containers on a flatbed: length, width, height, weight, and description. Dimensions use the units chosen in Settings.

5/10

Trip times and points

This screen only appears when you edit a saved trip from History. Here you can edit the start time, arrival time, cargo handling start and end times, and the addresses and odometer readings of the stops.

Any time that was not recorded during the trip is shown as a dash and cannot be edited: editing does not invent events that never happened.

6/10

Trip Summary

The summary shows the whole trip on one screen. Here you can also mark options: "Sleep in cabin", "Army mission", "Dangerous zone", "Temperature controlled", and "Working with crane" (if the truck has a crane).

"Edit" takes you back to the steps. The green "Start trip" button saves the trip and starts it. If you have a navigation app on your phone, the app will offer to open the address in it.

7/10

Arrival

When you reach the site, tap "Arrived". The app records the time and suggests an odometer reading.

"Start Unloading/Loading" starts the cargo handling timer, and "Stop Unloading/Loading" stops it. If something fails to save (for example, if the phone freezes), the app will tell you and ask you to tap again — nothing is ever saved half-done.

8/10

Unload/Load at this stop

If you deliver cargo in parts, mark what was delivered at this stop: fuel tanker sections, container percentage, pallets, or specific vehicles on a car transporter. The app remembers how much is left on board and will not let you unload more than you have.

9/10

What happened to the container?

After unloading, the app asks what happens next with the container: "Left here", "Still on the truck", going "To a client", or "Return" to a place (port, depot) or "Another address". Your answer sets up the next step of the trip.

10/10

Trip Summary

After a stop, choose "Continue Trip" (next stop on the same trip), "Finish Trip", or "New Trip".

"Fill in the next trip" lets you enter the next trip in advance while unloading — client, cargo, numbers. Then "Finish and drive" starts it right away.
