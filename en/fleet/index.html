Fleet

Fleet is for companies with several trucks and drivers. The fleet manager gives each driver a one-time code. With it, the phone gets a separate journal of the fleet with the fleet's truck and trailer; what you record there goes to the fleet. "My journal" — your own journal — stays as it is and stays yours.

1/6

Join a fleet with a code

On the first launch, tap "I drive for a fleet — enter code". Later: Settings → "Driver profile" → "Join a fleet with a code".

Enter the code from your fleet manager and tap "Join the fleet". If the manager sent you a link (my.vecturabook.com/fleet/join…), just open it on your phone: the app opens with the code already filled in. You can also paste the whole link into the "Code" field.

The code works only once and only for a limited time. If the app says the code is wrong, expired or already used, ask your fleet manager for a new one.

If the phone is already in another fleet, the app asks first: the new code sends the last records to that fleet and leaves it, and its journal stays on the phone for viewing only. Finish the trip and end the shift there first, otherwise the app says "Finish the current trip first." or "End the fleet shift first." Without internet nothing changes.

2/6

Two journals

After you join, the phone has two journals: "My journal" and the journal of the fleet. A strip at the top of every screen shows which one is open: blue-grey "My journal" or purple with the fleet's name. Tap the strip to pick the other one ("Which journal?"). You can switch only when no trip is started, otherwise "Finish the current trip first."

Shifts, trips and refuels of the fleet's journal go to the fleet automatically when there is internet; "Send to the fleet now" in "Driver profile" sends them right away. "My journal" never goes to the fleet — only to your own account. Each journal has its own shift, drafts and timers.

If the manager gives you another truck during a trip, the change comes after the trip ends.

3/6

Your own truck

If you drive for the fleet on your own truck, tap "Use my own truck in the fleet" in "Driver profile" and choose your truck ("Garage", "My journal"). It becomes the truck in the fleet's journal, and the fleet sees its plate on your trips.

Fuel for your own truck is your expense: its refuels always go to "My journal", even when the fleet's journal is open. If no shift of your own is open, "My journal" gets a short closed shift for the time you work for the fleet, so the refuel is in your reports and in your own account. Fuel for the fleet's truck and for the fleet trailer's unit goes to the fleet. A stop with both is saved as one: if one part fails, nothing is saved.

The odometer of your own truck runs on through both journals.

4/6

Leave the fleet

"Driver profile" → "Leave the fleet". End the fleet's shift first ("End the fleet shift first."). The app first sends the last records, then leaves. Without internet: "No connection to the server. Try again when you are online." — nothing changes.

After you leave, the fleet's journal stays on the phone for viewing only: the strip turns grey and says "… · view only". "Trip History", refuels, documents and PDF reports are there; buttons for changes are hidden and a shift cannot be started. Nothing more goes to the fleet. To come back, ask the manager for a new code.

5/6

Fleet for the owner

For the fleet manager. Turn on "Owner / Hired" in Settings → "Driver profile": the "Fleet" row appears there.

The fleet belongs to your VecturaBook account. If the phone is not signed in, the screen says "Sign in to your account first:" — tap "Connect to VecturaBook" and sign in with your account's email. If the account has no fleet, the screen offers "Create a fleet": enter "Fleet name" and tap "Create a fleet" — the free trial starts at once.

The screen shows "Drivers" — each with "Linked" or "Not linked yet", truck, trailer and the number of shifts, trips and refuels — and the fleet's "Garage". Below: "Add driver" with the "Driver's name" field, "+ Add Truck" ("Plate Number", "Truck Type", "Body Type") and "+ Add Trailer" ("Plate Number", compatibility, "Body Type"). The same can be done in your browser on my.vecturabook.com.

Without a subscription the screen shows "Fleet subscription" and "Drivers": tap a driver for "Get code" and "Send the code to the driver" — a new driver's phone joins the fleet without a subscription too.

A driver who left shows "Left the fleet" with the time instead of "Linked".

6/6

Driver

Tap a driver to open the card. "Truck" and "Trailer": choose from the fleet's garage and tap "Save". Only trailers that fit the chosen truck are offered. The driver's phone picks up the change by itself; during a trip — after the trip ends.

"Get code" gives a one-time code with the time it is valid until. "Send the code to the driver" sends the code and the link through a messenger. The code is shown only once. A new code moves the driver to a new phone: the old phone leaves the fleet.

Below are the driver's "Shifts", "Trips" and "Refuels" that have reached the fleet.

The card of a driver who left says "Left the fleet" with the time. A new code from you brings the driver back.
