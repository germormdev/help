VecturaBook — English

Online app

The online app is my.vecturabook.com: the same logbook in your browser and on your other devices.

1/2

Connect your phone

Settings → "Connect to VecturaBook". Scan the QR code from the web cabinet, open the cabinet in your browser and confirm on your phone, or enter the code shown on screen. "Disconnect this phone" unlinks it.

2/2

Sync

A connected phone automatically sends new entries and receives edits from your other devices. The status line shows "All synced" or how many entries are waiting to be sent. When offline, entries wait on your phone.
