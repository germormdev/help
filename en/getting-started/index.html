VecturaBook — English

Getting started

VecturaBook is a truck driver's logbook: shifts, trips, refuels, parking stops, documents, and vehicles on one phone. Everything stays on your phone and works offline.

1/5

First launch

When you open the app for the first time, it asks a few questions in four short steps. This takes about a minute, and you can change any of your answers later in Settings and Garage.

If you already have a VecturaBook backup (for example, from your old phone), tap "Restore from Backup" on the first step: your entire logbook will be restored, and you will not need to answer the questions.

2/5

Name, truck, trailer, permissions

Step 1 — your name. It is printed on reports. You can leave it blank and fill it in later.

Step 2 — your semi truck or rigid truck: plate number, truck type, and body type. You pick the type and body using pictures in a chain of questions: each answer brings up the next question.

Step 3 — trailer. You can skip it with the "Skip Trailer" button and add it later in Garage.

Step 4 — permissions: Camera (container numbers, seals, odometer), Location (your city at the start of a shift, only when you ask for it), and Notifications (timers for waiting, refueling, and unloading). You can decline any of them — the app will still work, just without that feature.

3/5

Main Screen

The Main Screen shows where you are in your workday: shift not started, shift open, trip in progress, at an unloading stop, and so on. The buttons on it always lead to the next step, so you cannot miss anything.

At the top is a bar with the menu button on the left and the "?" button on the right. The "?" button is on every screen: it briefly explains the screen, and tapping "More" opens the relevant section of this manual.

4/5

Menu

The three-line button opens the tile menu: Garage, Trip History, Report, Refuel Log, Maintenance, Vehicle Documents, Address Book, Help. At the bottom is Settings.

On any other screen, a back arrow replaces the menu button.

5/5

Offline help

The app downloads the text of this manual in its language and stores it on the phone, so "?" and "Help" work on the road without a signal. Once a day, when internet is available, the app quietly checks for updates.

If the help has never been downloaded yet, the app will ask you to connect to the internet.
