Refuelling, AdBlue, unit

Refuels are used to calculate fuel consumption, build reports, and show the "to reserve" estimate.

1/4

Refuel timer

Tapping "Refuel" on the driving screen starts a timer so the refueling time is logged. "Finish Refueling" stops it and opens the liters entry screen. The timer also runs in the notification shade if the app is minimized.

2/4

Liters and odometer

Enter diesel in liters, and if needed, AdBlue and fuel for the trailer unit (reefer, genset). The odometer reading is pre-filled, or you can scan it with the camera. "Save Refuel" saves everything as a single entry.

3/4

Trailer unit

If your trailer has a generator, turn on "Generator (genset) installed" in "Trailer settings". Then the driving screen gets a "Trailer unit running" switch, the app tracks unit hours and consumption in liters per hour, and the refuel screen shows a field for unit fuel.

4/4

Refuel Log

The log shows all refuels for the week, month, all time, or a custom period: "Total fuel", "Stops", and "Avg. consumption". The AdBlue share of diesel and trailer unit consumption are calculated separately.

"Generate PDF Report" creates a fuel report that you can share. Any refuel entry can be deleted.
