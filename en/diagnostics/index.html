Diagnostics channel

The diagnostic channel sends data from your phone to the developer so failures can be investigated on real data. It is off unless you switch it on yourself.

1/5

Register on the server

Read the consent text and, if you agree, tap "I agree, register this phone". "Revoke this phone" turns the channel off and erases the certificate.

2/5

What to send

Three switches: "Application activity logs", "Trip database", and "Container number photos". Each one is off until you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time. While all three are off, nothing is sent. The app does not read your photo gallery.

3/5

Warning

Opening the service screens starts with a warning. You can leave right away by tapping "Get me out of here".

4/5

Channel server

Connection status: on air, silent, no network, or server not accepting. Everything ready to send waits on your phone — there is nothing you need to do.

5/5

Channel bundle

For company or test phones, a channel bundle can be installed from a password-protected .p12 file. The password is used once and is never saved.
